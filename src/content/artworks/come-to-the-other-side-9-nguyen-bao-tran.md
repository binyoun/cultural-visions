artistStatement: |-
  Drawing inspiration from the visual concept of "slices of life", the 10-image series "Come to the Other Side" reinterprets the mundane as a gateway to a parallel existence. Rather than simply documenting daily life, the series functions as an enticing portal, inviting viewers to experience the world through a fresh, chaotic perspective. Rooted in the frenetic energy of downtown Ho Chi Minh City, the work reflects the abrupt rhythms, traffic motion, and repetitive cycles of the urban environment, capturing the profound human desire to escape reality for a parallel world.

  Image #9 marks the climax of this strange adventure, representing a moment of newfound stability. As the viewer becomes accustomed to the "other side," the previously fractured elements of the city begin to reconnect, resolving into a balanced and specific form. This piece visualizes a harmonious convergence where the two worlds, our known reality and the parallel digital existence, begin to merge into one.

  The work operates as a synthesis of street photography and creative digital manipulation, conveying a distinct Digital Age perspective. Presented strictly in black and white, the series strips away the distraction of color, directing the viewer's focus toward shapes, motion, and the underlying patterns of life rather than the literal clarity of the subjects.
longBio: |-
  Nguyen Bao Tran is a 2D visual communicator and digital explorer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. As a concept-driven designer, she specializes in translating the noise of contemporary life into clear and impactful visual signals.

  Operating at the intersection of structured design and "organized chaos," Tran balances a meticulous eye for detail with a playful willingness to break the grid. Her designs are not only functional but evocative and narrative-led, creating visual experiences that are deeply reflective of the modern human experience.

  Her portfolio includes recognition in academic and international showcases, including the Pingyao International Photography Festival, the "Từ Đô" Typography Exhibition, and the RMIT Experience Day showcases.
