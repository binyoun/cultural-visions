artistStatement: |-
  This assignment happened at the same time my family was traveling to Sa Pa, so I decided to create a photo series comparing the two cities connected to my life: Sa Pa and Ha Noi. Sa Pa, known as "the city of fog", is located high in the mountains and surrounded by clouds and mist. Meanwhile, Ha Noi, often called "the city for peace", is a modern city located in the plains. Even though the two cities are very different in geography, atmosphere, and lifestyle, they also share similarities in culture and daily life. Through this project, I wanted to explore both the contrasts and connections between these two places through photography.

  This series captures the contrast between Sa Pa and Ha Noi through their environments. Sa Pa appears quiet, distant, and covered in fog and mountains, while Ha Noi feels dense, energetic, and constantly moving. Together, the landscapes and cityscapes reveal two very different atmospheres.
