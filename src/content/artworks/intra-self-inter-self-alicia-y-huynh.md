artistStatement: |-
  When it comes to personal identity, one tends to understand the concept as a collection of an individual's unique characteristics separating them from others. However, from my perspective, this view is incomplete, for it neglects another equally important side: that beneath our uniqueness lies a sense of commonality that connects us together. As it turns out, the idea of personal identity possesses a dualistic nature: each of us is different, yet we are alike in numerous ways; each of us is our own person, yet we could not exist without the presence of others, like a tree cannot become a tree without its roots.

  Following this idea, my project, INTRA-SELF, INTER-SELF (Within a Self, Among the Self), aims to explore this entangled relationship between the "Self" and the "Others". It attempts to understand key questions: what would happen when there exists an imbalance between the two sides of one's identity? How would they differ or stay similar? Will there be a way out? What began as a psychological investigation evolved into a meditative journey for me, reflecting upon my life-long experiences, which I channelled into this project. Distinctiveness and similarity need not be opposing forces; instead, they are complementary in making up oneself.
