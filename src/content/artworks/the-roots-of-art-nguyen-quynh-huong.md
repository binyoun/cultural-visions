artistStatement: |-
  This project explores the roots of art by reinterpreting Vietnamese lacquer painting through photography and digital collage. I use raw photographs as visual materials, capturing fragments of nature, urban structures, and textures, and layer them to mimic the flatness, warmth, and material depth of traditional lacquer works. I collected raw photographs from my immediate surroundings, my backyard, parks, and city streets, focusing on close-up textures rather than polished images. In post-production, I cut, mask, and layer different photographic textures to construct each composition, then adjust color and tone to reference the palette of lacquer painting, red, black, brown, and gold, while preserving the photographic textures underneath.
