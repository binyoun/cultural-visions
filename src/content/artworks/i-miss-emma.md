artistStatement: |-
  I was born in Phan Thiet, but I did not grow up here. Even so, every summer I was able to return to this place. I love everything about it, the smell of the sea, the scent of the wind, the sight of fishermen at work, the aroma of homemade fish sauce, and the memories and childhood moments, because I used to come back here more often when I was younger. The image of the fishing village at five or six in the morning stays with me: the lively sounds of people, fishermen heading out to sea, and boats clustered closely together, creating a sense of connection and quiet beauty.

  Emma is the name I adopted from my baptismal name, Marie Emmanuel Tran Dang Minh Anh. I began using this name regularly in sixth grade, and it eventually became my artistic name. As a child, my mother often described me as playful and quick-witted, always smiling brightly. As I grew older, I became less innocent and carefree than before. Sometimes my mother says she misses the image of my younger self, and in truth, I miss that version of myself too.

  "i miss" is a project that I am deeply connected to. It expresses my emotions and affection for the places and people I grew up with. In terms of technical skills, I mainly worked with layered images placed over one another. I believe this technique best represents longing, the sense of remembering and forgetting, where memories overlap and merge into one another.
