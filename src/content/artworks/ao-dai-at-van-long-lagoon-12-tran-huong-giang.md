  The "ao dai" is a cultural heritage, a symbol of beauty for every Vietnamese woman. This photography project explores the beauty and cultural significance of the Vietnamese ao dai through a series of images taken at Van Long Lagoon in Ninh Binh. By placing the traditional garment within the natural landscape of Northern Vietnam, limestone mountains, rivers, and ancient architecture, the project highlights the harmony between Vietnamese heritage and nature.
