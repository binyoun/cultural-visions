  Continuing from the overall concept of appreciating the hard work and sacrifices of ordinary people, this project expands from sanitation workers into the city's nighttime activities and atmosphere: street cleaners, market workers, delivery drivers, security guards, and many other unnoticed workers who keep the city functioning long after the sun goes down. What interested me most was the contrast between the silence of the night and the physical exhaustion of these workers; even as the streets become emptier and calmer, their work does not stop.
