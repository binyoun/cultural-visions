artistStatement: |-
  My inspiration for this project came from the symbolic culture of ancestor worship in the Lunar New Year season. Rather than viewing it purely as a spiritual practice, I'm more interested in how this tradition sustains the intergenerational connection among Vietnamese families. I chose the documentary genre combined with the still life genre to capture my grandmother's preparation process for the family altar. Through her thoughtfulness and care, the project aims to highlight the role of old people as living connectors, reinforcing a sense of family continuity from past to present.
